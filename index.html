About the event

A 24-hour national-level hackathon

HACKATHON 2026 is hosted by St. Peter’s Engineering College to bring students together to build technology-driven solutions. It encourages innovation and practical problem-solving, promotes teamwork, creativity and engineering skills, and provides a platform for interdisciplinary collaboration.
